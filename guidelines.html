Community Guidelines

Last updated: November 23, 2026

These guidelines keep the site friendly and safe for everyone, many of whom are young. They apply to comments, profiles, messages, pack uploads and anything else you do here, and they are part of the Terms of Service.

1. Be kind

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or slurs, or attacks on people for who they are.
  • No sexual or pornographic content, nudity, graphic violence or gore. Nothing sexual involving minors, ever.
  • No sharing other people’s private information (doxxing), and no pressuring anyone to share theirs.
  • Disagree without being cruel. If someone asks you to stop, stop.

2. Be honest

  • No impersonation. Don’t pose as me, Glacier, Mojang, Microsoft, Minecraft staff, other servers or creators, and don’t claim to be “official”, “verified” or “approved by an admin”.
  • No scams, fake giveaways (“free Minecoins”, gift cards), phishing, or begging for money in a misleading way.
  • No spam, chain messages or advertising that has nothing to do with the site.

3. Messages

  • Don’t send unwanted links, ads or repeated messages. Messages are limited so nobody can flood others.
  • Never ask a younger person for personal details, photos or to move to another app to chat privately.
  • If someone bothers you: use Block in the conversation, then report it (see section 6).

4. Uploading packs

  • Only Minecraft-related packs and add-ons, in the right category, with an honest name and description.
  • Only upload things you made or have permission to share. Don’t re-upload other people’s work, paid content or leaked content.
  • No malware or hidden code. Resource packs can’t contain scripts, and script packs must do what they say. Nothing that sends player data to webhooks, steals accounts, or hides what it does.
  • No link shorteners, ad links or monetization tricks inside packs or descriptions. The site adds download links itself.
  • No keyword stuffing, misleading icons or screenshots, or claims that aren’t true.
  • Every upload is checked automatically before it goes live. Changing the name, icon or links of a live pack sends it back for a check.

5. Play fair

Don’t use bots, scripts or “bypass” tools to inflate downloads, ratings, followers or comments, to skip download pages, or to attack the site. Don’t make extra accounts to get around a ban or to boost yourself.

6. Reporting a problem

  • Comments: use the report button on the comment.
  • Packs: use the “Report a problem” button on the pack page.
  • Messages or anything else: join the Discord, open a ticket or message the owner, and include the username and a screenshot.
  • Copyright: see Copyright & DMCA.

If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

7. What happens if the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, I may remove content, reject a pack, limit messaging, suspend or ban an account, and where required, report it to the authorities. I don’t have to warn you first. If you think a decision was a mistake, you can appeal through the Discord and I’ll take another look.
