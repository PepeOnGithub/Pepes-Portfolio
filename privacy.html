Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Pepe’s Portfolio (the website at pepe.glacierclient.xyz, run by Pepe / Glacier Productions, “I” or “me”) collects, why, who can see it, and what choices you have. It covers the website, the pack library, community uploads, accounts, comments, ratings, follows and messages. It does not cover other websites I link to (see “Other services”).

The short version
  • You can browse the site without an account.
  • If you make an account, I store your name, email, profile and what you post.
  • Direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • I don’t sell your data and I don’t run advertising or analytics trackers on this site.
  • Downloads go through ad-supported link pages (Linkvertise and/or LootLabs) that have their own privacy policies.
  • You must be at least 13 to make an account.

1. Information I collect

Just by visiting

  • Technical data. Like any website, the companies that host and protect it (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) see your IP address, browser and device details and the pages you request in their normal server logs.
  • Download and view counts. To count each visitor once a day, the uploads service keeps a one-way scrambled value (a hash) built from your IP address and the pack. It is deleted after about two days. The counts themselves are not linked to you. You can turn off download counting from your device in Settings.
  • Ratings. A rating is stored with a random ID that your browser makes up (a “voter ID”), not with your name.
  • Settings. Your theme, favorites, recently viewed packs and similar preferences stay in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to me.

When you create an account

  • Sign in with Google or GitHub: your account ID, name, profile picture and email address from that service.
  • Sign up with email: your email address, a display name, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, never as plain text.
  • Sign-in session: a sign-in token, kept in your browser’s local storage and on my server for up to 30 days or until you sign out.
  • Profile details you add: username, display name, profile picture, banner, bio, links and featured YouTube videos.

What you post or do

  • Comments, replies, likes and reports on comments; ratings.
  • Who you follow, who follows you, and who you block.
  • Direct messages you send (text only).
  • Packs you upload: the files, icon, name, description, tags, changelog, showcase video link and version history.
  • Creator earnings details if you add them: your Linkvertise user ID, and/or your LootLabs API token. The token is stored encrypted, is only used to create download links for your packs, and is never shown back to you or anyone else.

If you contact me

Anything you send me on Discord or elsewhere (reports, appeals, requests), plus the details needed to deal with it.

2. How I use it

  • To run the site: sign you in, show your profile, comments and uploads, deliver your messages and show download counts.
  • To keep it safe: review uploads and profiles, handle reports, stop spam, scams, malware and abuse, and enforce the Terms and Community Guidelines.
  • To make download links: build Linkvertise and LootLabs links for packs (see “Other services”).
  • To meet legal obligations and protect my rights.

Legal bases (EEA/UK). Where GDPR applies, I rely on: running your account and the features you ask for (contract); security, abuse prevention and basic statistics (legitimate interests); legal obligations; and your consent where you click through to third-party content. You can complain to your local data protection authority.

3. Automated review of uploads and profiles

Uploads and creator profiles are checked automatically before they appear. First by fixed rules, then by an AI model. To do that, the following is sent to Google’s Gemini API: the pack’s name, category, description, tags, uploader name, changelog, list of files, text files such as manifests and scripts, and its icon; and, for creator profiles, your username, bio, links and featured video links. Google processes this under its own terms. The review can approve or reject automatically. If you think a decision is wrong, ask me and I’ll look at it by hand. I don’t send your email address, password or messages to the AI reviewer.

4. What is public and what is private

Public: your username, display name, profile picture, banner, bio, links and videos; your approved packs; your comments and replies; your followers and who you follow (and their counts); whether you have a verified badge.

Private: your email address, password hash, sign-in tokens, LootLabs token and your direct messages (visible only to you and the person you wrote to, apart from what is described in section 5).

5. Direct messages

Messages are stored on my servers and are not end-to-end encrypted, so I can technically read them. I don’t read them as a matter of routine. I may look at messages where it is needed to deal with a report or abuse, fix a security problem, or respond to a legal request. The person you message can copy, screenshot or share what you send. You can block anyone from messaging you. Don’t share passwords, addresses, phone numbers or other personal information in messages.

6. Who I share information with

I don’t sell your personal information and I don’t share it for advertising. I use these service providers to run the site:

  • Cloudflare: hosts the accounts and uploads services, the database and file storage, and sits in front of the website as a security and speed layer.
  • GitHub: hosts the website (GitHub Pages) and, if you choose it, signs you in.
  • Google: signs you in (if you choose it), provides the AI review (Gemini) and serves the site’s fonts.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect the safety of users or the public, or to protect my rights. If the site is ever transferred to someone else, your information would go with it, and this policy would continue to apply until updated.

7. Other services you may meet

When your browser loads something from another company, that company receives your IP address and device details and handles them under its own policy.

  • Linkvertise and LootLabs. Pack downloads go through one of these ad-supported link pages. They show ads, may use cookies and tracking, and collect their own data. Read their policies before you continue, and never enter payment or personal details on those pages.
  • YouTube. Video thumbnails load from YouTube’s image servers. A video only plays (and loads YouTube’s player, from youtube-nocookie.com) after you click it.
  • Images and GIFs in messages. If a message contains a GIF or image, your browser loads it from Tenor, Giphy, Imgur or Discord’s servers.
  • Fonts and icons. Loaded from Google Fonts and Cloudflare’s cdnjs.
  • Links out. Discord, YouTube, GitHub, social networks, PayPal and other sites I link to.

8. Cookies and local storage

I don’t use advertising or analytics cookies. The site uses your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your settings. Cloudflare may set a security cookie, and Linkvertise, LootLabs, YouTube and other third parties may set their own. You can clear site data in your browser at any time. That signs you out and resets your settings.

9. How long I keep it

  • Account, profile, comments, ratings, follows and messages: until you ask me to delete your account.
  • Uploaded packs: until you delete them from My uploads or I remove them.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Scrambled visitor values for counting: about 2 days.
  • Reports and moderation notes: as long as needed to keep the site safe.
  • Backups and logs: short-term copies kept by my hosting providers under their own policies.

10. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, delete your packs and comments, block people, sign out, and clear your browser data yourself. You can also ask me to:

  • tell you what I hold about you, or send you a copy;
  • correct or delete it, including deleting your account;
  • stop using it for something I do on the basis of legitimate interests.

Contact me through the Pepe Discord server (open a ticket or message the owner). I may need to confirm the account is yours, and I aim to answer within 30 days. Account deletion is currently done by hand by me. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California) you may have additional rights under local law, and I will honor them. I do not sell or “share” personal information as those terms are used in California law.

11. Children

The site is not for children under 13 and you must be at least 13 to create an account. If you are older than 13 but under 18, make sure a parent or guardian is okay with you using accounts and messages. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If I learn that I have, I’ll delete it. If you think a child under 13 has an account, tell me. Many people who play Minecraft are young, so please be careful: never share personal details in messages, and block and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.

12. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are salted and hashed, and LootLabs tokens are encrypted. No website can be completely secure, so I can’t promise absolute security. Use a strong, unique password.

13. Where your information is processed

My providers run servers in many countries, including the United States. By using the site you understand your information may be processed outside your own country.

14. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy I’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, show a notice on the site. Using the site after a change means you accept it.

15. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: the Pepe Discord server (open a ticket or message the owner).
