Copyright & DMCA

Last updated: November 23, 2026

I respect other people’s work and expect the same from everyone who uploads here. This page explains how to report content that infringes your copyright and how someone can respond.

1. Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe a pack or other content on the site infringes your copyright, send me a notice through the Pepe Discord server (open a ticket or message the owner) with the following:

  1. Your name and a way to contact you (an email address or Discord username).
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed.
  3. The address (URL) of the pack or page on the site that contains the material, with enough detail for me to find it.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use isn’t authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is fine).

Please don’t send a notice unless you mean it. Knowingly making a false claim can make you legally responsible for damages.

2. What I do

When I get a valid notice I may remove or disable access to the material, tell the person who uploaded it, and give them a copy of your notice (including your contact details).

3. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake or that you have the right to use the material, you can send me a counter-notice with:

  1. Your name and contact details.
  2. What was removed and where it was before it was removed.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. A statement that you agree to the jurisdiction of the courts where you live (or, if you’re outside the United States, anywhere the site’s operator can be found) and that you will accept legal papers from the person who sent the original notice.
  5. Your signature.

If the original claimant doesn’t tell me within 10 business days that they’ve started a court action, I may restore the material.

4. Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly upload infringing material will be closed.

5. Trademarks and other complaints

Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang AB and Microsoft; this site is not approved by or associated with them. For trademark, privacy or other legal complaints, contact me the same way and tell me what the problem is and where it is on the site.
